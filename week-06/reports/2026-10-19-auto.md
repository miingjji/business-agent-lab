## 2. Findings

1. **autumn-sale: clicks and purchases fell while spend and impressions stayed flat.**
   Spend was $780 in both weeks and impressions moved only from 52,000 to 51,000, but clicks fell from 1,300 to 1,020 and purchases from 39 to 25. Revenue fell from $2,730 to $1,750. Revenue per purchase stayed at $70, so the drop comes from fewer clicks and a lower conversion rate, not from smaller orders. Cost per purchase rose from $20.00 to $31.20 and ROAS fell from 3.50 to 2.24.
   - Did the creative, offer or sale terms change, or did the sale reach an end date or a stock limit?
   - Did the audience see the same ads too often, so fewer people clicked (CTR -0.50 pp)?
   - Did the landing page, checkout or purchase tracking change or break, which would explain the lower conversion rate (-0.55 pp)?
   - Did a competitor's promotion or the other campaigns reduce interest in the sale?

The other campaigns moved little. retargeting (ROAS +0.15, 26 to 27 purchases) and brand-search (unchanged ROAS of 8.00) changed by amounts within normal week-to-week variation, and new-arrivals stayed at about the same low level as the previous week (ROAS 1.53 to 1.56, 12 purchases in both weeks), so they are not listed as changes to investigate.
