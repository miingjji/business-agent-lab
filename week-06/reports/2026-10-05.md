## 2. Findings

1. **new-arrivals: CTR halved and ROAS fell to 1.00.**
   Impressions rose from 30,000 to 32,000 and spend from $540 to $560, but clicks fell from 900 to 480 and purchases from 18 to 8. Revenue ($560) now equals spend.
   - Did the creative, headline or image change in the week of 2026-10-05, or has the audience seen it too often?
   - Did targeting or placements change so the ads reach people less likely to click?
   - Did a competitor, a seasonal shift or the autumn-sale campaign draw attention away from this one?
   - Is the lower conversion rate (-0.33 pp) caused by the landing page or product availability, or is it only noise on a small number of purchases (8)?

2. **retargeting: conversion rate and ROAS rose with unchanged CTR.**
   Clicks rose only from 480 to 500, but purchases rose from 20 to 24, and revenue per purchase rose from $90 to $100. ROAS went from 3.00 to 4.00.
   - Did the retargeting audience change, for example a larger pool of recent visitors or cart abandoners?
   - Did the higher revenue per purchase come from a few larger orders, or from a price or bundle change?
   - Did the new-arrivals drop mean fewer first-time clicks, so retargeting was credited for purchases that other campaigns started earlier?
